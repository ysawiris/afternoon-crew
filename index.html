Why The Reset

PM Crew, on purpose

PM Crew is 15 minutes that belong to your crew. Lately it has drifted into free time: kids wandering, crews split up, nobody sure who is where. That ends now. Every part of PM Crew is intentional: it has a plan, and everyone knows it.

What PM Crew drifted into

  • Kids walking out before the crew is checked in
  • Every grade outside at once, crews mixed and scattered
  • Hallways and buildings used as hangout spots
  • "Recess" energy: no plan, no crew, no leader

What PM Crew is meant to be

  • Your crew, together, with your crew leader
  • A check-in that actually happens, every day
  • Outside time that is earned and scheduled by grade
  • A plan for the day, whether you're in or out
Intentional means: you know your day, you know your room, and your crew leader knows where you are.

Say It Right

It's not recess.
It's Crew Time.

Crew Time is 2:20 to 2:35, every day, with your crew. Some days your crew spends it outside. It is still Crew Time: you check in first, you stay with your crew, and your crew leader knows where you are.

Hear someone say "recess"? Fix it: "It's Crew Time."

On Your Grade's Day

Check in, then out together

1

Check in

Straight to your crew room at 2:20. Whole crew checked in within 2 minutes.

2

Out as a crew

Your crew leader walks the crew out together. Nobody heads out on their own.

3

Your time

Friends, water, bathroom, talk to teachers. On the blacktop, not in the buildings.

4

2:35, done

Balls held, no stops. Walk to 6th and be ready before the 2:40 bell.

Crew can't check everyone in within 2 minutes? No outside Crew Time that day, and the crew does a crew reflection.

Not Your Grade's Day

Stay in. Do the EL Crew Lesson

On days your grade isn't outside, Crew Time is a lesson, not a break. Your crew stays in its room with your crew leader and works through the EL Crew Lesson for the full 15 minutes.

1

Check in

Straight to your crew room at 2:20. Same 2-minute check-in as every day.

2

Crew Lesson

Your crew leader runs the EL Crew Lesson. Everyone participates and completes it.

3

Stay put, then 6th

Bathroom or water only with your crew leader's OK. At 2:35, lesson done, walk to 6th.

Inside Crew Time is

  • Whole crew in the room, on the same lesson
  • EL Crew Lesson finished before 2:35

Inside Crew Time is not

  • The hallway, the blacktop, or "just for a minute"
  • Another crew's room, the MS building, or the Admin building
A crew found outside or in the halls on a day that isn't theirs loses its next outside day and does a crew reflection.
