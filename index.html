Getting To Crew

Straight to crew. Check in.

Do

  • Go directly to your crew — no stops, no detours
  • Check in so your crew knows you're there
  • Go outside with your crew once everyone is checked in

Don't

  • Walk friends to their crew — you'll see them at recess
  • Go to the bathroom — bathroom time is recess
  • Stop to talk to teachers — teachers are around at recess
There is no transition time — crew starts at 2:20 sharp. Check in within 2 minutes, and get your whole crew outside.
If the crew can't get everyone checked in within 2 minutes: no outside recess that day, and the crew completes a crew reflection — talk about what went wrong, make a plan to fix it.
Crew time starts at 2:20 whether you're there or not. The longer you take, the more recess you take from your crew.

The 6th-Period Bell Rings — Which One Are You?

Ready vs. tardy

Ready
  • In your seat
  • Materials out on your desk
  • Ready to start when the 6th-period bell rings
Tardy (Late)
  • Still in the hallway
  • In your seat but your materials are not out
  • Sitting in your seat chatting when the 6th-period bell rings (yes, that is tardy)
Being in the room isn't the same as being ready. Seat + materials out + done talking = on time.
